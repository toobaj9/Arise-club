MAR 31

🌙 De-stress Night - Research Murder Mystery

📅 March 31, 2026 • 5:30 - 7:00 PM EST

📍 BV 361

ARISE x UPDSS hosted an interactive murder mystery experience where students stepped into the role of forensic investigators. Participants analyzed evidence, questioned suspects, and uncovered how bias can influence case outcomes through engaging, hands-on stations like DNA clues and interrogation tactics. The event combined critical thinking, real research concepts, and a fun break from finals, with free pizza and snacks provided.

Critical Thinking Interactive Research
MAR 20

🎤 Women In STEM Resarch Panel

📅 March 20, 2026 • 7:00 - 8:00 PM EST

📍 Virtual (Zoom)

ARISE, in collaboration with SPARK Health, hosted a Women in STEM Research Panel in celebration of International Women’s Month. This virtual event brought together inspiring women from diverse fields across research and industry to share their academic journeys, professional experiences, and advice for students interested in STEM. Attendees had the opportunity to hear from experts in quantum computing, epidemiology, biomedical engineering, and clinical research, and to engage in meaningful discussions about career pathways, challenges, and opportunities in STEM.

Panel Research Opportunities Faculty
FEB 13

👔 Kickstart Research Pack: Interview Edition

📅 February 13, 2026 • 3:30 - 5:30 PM EST

📍 BV 264

ARISE hosted the Kickstart Research Pack: Interview Edition, an interactive workshop designed to help students prepare for research-based interviews. This in-person session focused on providing practical interview tips, exploring common research interview questions, and engaging participants in mock interview scenarios to build confidence and readiness. Attendees also had the opportunity to connect and network with students who have prior research experience, gaining valuable insights into academic and professional pathways in research. Light refreshments were provided to create a welcoming and collaborative environment for learning and discussion.

Interview Tips Research Network Free
